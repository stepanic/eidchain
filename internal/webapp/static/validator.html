Register your node's key

On the machine that runs your node:

eidchain keygen
eidchain validator request --server URL

Paste the record it prints below and sign it with your eID card (PIN2). The card is needed only now: from then on your node signs blocks with its own key. Your key counts toward the authorities once it has signed a block.

One person, one vote. You may run validating nodes in several places, each with its own key, registered one by one; never copy a key to a second node. Your personal ID number identifies you, so all your keys count as one person: they take turns with other people, not with each other. A key stops being a validator when the certificate that registered it expires; registering it again with a new card renews it. To drop a key, use eidchain validator retire KEY-ID.

This is public and permanent. Your name is shown next to your key. The certificate in the record also holds your personal ID number (OIB); the explorer hides it, but anyone who downloads the block can read it.
